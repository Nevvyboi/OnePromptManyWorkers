The things people ask

Will it send something I would be embarrassed by?

Not without you seeing it. Every email waits for your approval by default, and you can rewrite any of it. Once you trust a ladder you can let it send on its own, and most people do that after the second invoice.

Does it sound like a robot wrote it?

It learns from the reminders you have already sent, so the fourth one sounds like you on a firm day rather than like a collections agency. If a line sounds wrong, changing it teaches it.

What if the client actually cannot pay?

Then the ladder is the wrong tool and it will say so. When a client replies asking for time, Fortnight pauses that invoice and hands the conversation back to you rather than sending reminder four into a difficult moment.

Do you touch my bank account?

No. Fortnight reads invoices and sends email. It has no payment permissions, cannot move money, and will never ask for banking details.
